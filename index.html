Each shift lasts 5 minutes. Out-earn the three rival miners, and only ore you sell at the station counts. Hold to fly toward your finger or cursor (or use WASD / arrows); your laser mines by itself. Watch your fuel.

Fly back to the station to sell ore and buy upgrades.

Field guide

Deeper pays more. Rocks near the station are mostly copper. Push past 8 km for ice, 16 km for gold, and 26 km if you want the purple stuff.

Know your rivals. Pip plays it safe near home. Juno chases the deep, rich rock. Vex tails you and grabs ore you knock loose. They all learn which depths pay best and upgrade as they earn.

Beat the clock. Shifts last 5 minutes and only sold ore counts, so get home before time runs out. Winning a shift pays a bonus. Thrusting burns fuel, and the station refuels you. Run dry and you limp home on reserve thrusters.

Mind your speed. Ramming a rock dents the hull. If the hull hits zero you're towed home and the cargo is lost.
